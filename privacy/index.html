Weilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Weilo is a weight plan app for iPhone and iPad. It works without an account and without a server. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

What the app keeps

Your plan (start, goal, dates, weigh-in day, height and sex if you enter them), your weigh-ins, body fat and body measurements, and your settings. All of it is stored on your device only.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off until you turn it on. If you do, Weilo reads weight, body fat percentage, waist circumference and height, so you don't have to type them. If you also allow saving, Weilo writes the weight, body fat and waist you log. You choose each type in the permission screen and can change it at any time in the Health app.

Health data is used only to show your plan and progress. It is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, is not given to anyone, and is not uploaded or stored in iCloud by the app.

No tracking

There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party code in the app. Weilo does not connect to the internet by itself. Links you tap, such as the sources in Settings, open in your browser.

Sharing and backups

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the app's data is part of that backup, which is handled by Apple and controlled by you.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. “Delete plan and all entries” in Settings erases your plan and every entry; deleting the app removes everything it stored. Values saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Turning Apple Health off in Weilo, or removing its access in the Health app, withdraws your permission.

Not medical advice

Weilo's plan, BMI and body fat figures are simple calculations. They are not a diagnosis. Please talk to a doctor before making medical decisions.

Children

Weilo is not directed at children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/akhundovb-sudo/weilo. Issues are public, so please leave out personal or health details.
