Weilo Support

Weilo: Weight Tracker for iPhone and iPad

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/akhundovb-sudo/weilo and say what you did, what you expected and which device and iOS version you use. Issues are public, so please leave out personal or health details.

Change or restart the plan

Settings → Start and Settings → Goal change the plan you have; it recalculates when you save. “Start a new plan” closes the current one and keeps your entries.

Fix or delete an entry

Open Plan → Daily and tap the day, or tap a measurement in Journal. The trash button deletes it.

Connect Apple Health

Settings → Apple Health. If nothing arrives, check the permissions in the Health app: your profile → Apps → Weilo.

Change the language

Settings → Language opens the app's page in iOS Settings, where you choose it.

Delete everything

Settings → Delete plan and all entries. Values saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Weilo's plan, BMI and body fat figures are simple calculations. Please talk to a doctor before making medical decisions.
